> Coverage planners for agricultural UAVs are usually ranked by path length. On a rest-to-rest
> boustrophedon sweep, two exclusion zones can **shorten the plan and still cost more energy**,
> because every extra cell adds stops and transits. This repository measures that gap in closed
> loop, with a C-code-generated NMPC and linear baselines flying the plans.

The manuscript is [`paper/paper_final.pdf`](paper/paper_final.pdf) (6 pages). Every number and
Tables I–II are regenerated by the scripts in this repository from the recorded data in `paper/data/`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **All results are simulation.** There is no flight test and no execution on embedded hardware;
> "embedded-deployable" refers to the generated C code, not to a measured deployment. Both energies
> are model outputs, not measured battery consumption. The paper was accepted at HRFEST 2026;
> publication in IEEE Xplore is pending and no DOI exists yet.

## ✨ Main results

Tracking RMSE [m] over 360 missions (30 seeds per cell, mean ± s.d.; Table III of the paper),
Ornstein–Uhlenbeck force disturbance of intensity σ:

| σ | PID | LQR | NMPC | NMPC vs best baseline |
| --- | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| 0.00 | 0.181 ± 0.000 | 0.167 ± 0.000 | **0.013 ± 0.000** | −92.2 % |
| 0.30 | 0.191 ± 0.005 | 0.209 ± 0.010 | **0.083 ± 0.007** | −56.7 % |
| 0.55 | 0.212 ± 0.009 | 0.226 ± 0.011 | **0.101 ± 0.007** | −52.4 % |
| 0.80 | 0.242 ± 0.012 | 0.251 ± 0.013 | **0.124 ± 0.008** | −48.8 % |

- **Length understates the energy cost of exclusions.** On a 20 × 14 m field two exclusion zones
  change the plan length by −4.5 to +3.4 % but raise its energy by 8.3–13.6 % (median 11.1 %) over
  299 generic positions (8.0 % at the reference one). A larger value, 22.6 %, arises only from a
  degenerate decomposition when scan rows lie exactly on inflated obstacle edges.
- **Over 600 random plans** the median increases are 3.2 % in length and 8.8 % in energy.
- **The planning ranking survives closed-loop flight** in the cases tested; the exclusion premium
  measured in closed loop is 7.4–9.1 % against 8.0 % planned.
- **Part of the NMPC's advantage is information.** An ablation attributes 35–42 % of the margin to its
  reference preview; with reference-acceleration feedforward, PID tracks better and LQR worse than the
  NMPC without preview.
- **Planned clearance is not executed clearance.** Every controller comes closer to the no-fly
  polygons than the planned 0.5 m, even without disturbance, and the result can depend on the
  traversal order of plans with equal planned cost.

## 🏗️ How the study is built

| Layer | Path | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Planner | `src/mission_planner/mission_planner/coverage_planner.py` | Boustrophedon decomposition, routed row links and transits, Held–Karp cell ordering, span merging; regression tests in `src/mission_planner/test/` |
| Plant and control | `nmpc_model/` | 18-state multirotor model (CasADi), NMPC optimal control problem for acados, PID and LQR baselines |
| Experiments | `paper/scripts/mc_experiments.py`, `baselines_ff.py` | Monte-Carlo harness: RK4 plant, NMPC and baselines, Ornstein–Uhlenbeck wind, delay buffer, metrics; feedforward baselines for the ablation |
| Planning studies | `paper/scripts/power_model.py`, `coverage_study.py` | Planning energy model, obstacle-position sweep and the 600 random plans |
| Paper | `paper/scripts/make_numbers.py`, `make_figures.py`, `fig_clearance.py`, `paper/` | Numbers, Tables I–II, figures and the LaTeX source |

Modules in `nmpc_model/` are the subset of the authors' earlier NMPC work that this paper needs.
`src/uav_nmpc_controller` (C++ ROS 2 node), `src/crop_uav_simulator` (Gazebo Classic field),
`src/multimodal_perception` and `mission_artifacts/` belong to the ROS 2 platform and are not used
for the paper's results.

## 🚀 Quick start

```bash
make setup     # virtual environment and dependencies
make test      # planner regression tests
make verify    # data checksums; regenerate numbers and Tables I-II and compare with the committed files
make paper     # both PDF profiles into paper/ (needs TeX Live)
```

Run `make help` for every target. The Monte-Carlo campaigns also need acados 0.6.0 (commit
`5033648`) built from source; pass its path as `make monte-carlo ACADOS=<path>`.

## 🔁 Reproducibility

| Paper element | Produced by | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Numbers in the text (`paper/numbers.tex`) | `make_numbers.py` from `paper/data/*.json` | Regenerated byte for byte |
| Tables I–II (`tab_coverage.tex`, `tab_clearance.tex`) | `make_numbers.py` | Regenerated byte for byte |
| Table III (RMSE) | Typed in `main.tex` | Checked against `paper/data/mc_main.json` (all 12 cells) |
| Fig. 1 architecture | `paper/fig_architecture.tex` (TikZ) | Illustration |
| Figs. 2–4 | `make_figures.py fig_coverage`, `fig_delay`, `fig_clearance.py` | Regenerated, same content |
| Coverage study (600 plans) | `coverage_study.py --jobs 3` | Deterministic, seed in the script |
| Monte-Carlo campaigns | `mc_experiments.py` (acados, CPU-hours) | Re-runs reproduced Table III to three decimals |

Every episode in the JSON files records its seed, and `paper/data/SHA256SUMS` holds the checksums
of the recorded data. CI runs the tests and checks that the numbers and Tables I–II regenerate
unchanged.

Recorded data in `paper/data/`:

| File | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `mc_main.json`, `mc_main_rerun.json` | 360-mission tracking campaign (plant at 100 Hz) and its re-run with the versioned script |
| `mc_delay.json`, `mc_delay_rerun.json`, `delay_inversions.json` | Actuation-delay sweep (10 seeds per delay, plant at 1 kHz), its re-run and the attitude-inversion counts |
| `mc_exclusions.json` | Closed loop on the exclusion plan: planned and executed energy and clearance |
| `mc_fair_controllers.json` | Ablation with feedforward baselines and the NMPC without preview |
| `coverage_study.json` | Table I, obstacle-position sweep and 600 random plans |
| `coverage_shift_submitted_planner.json` | Obstacle sweep with a planner without span merging (dashed curve of Fig. 2c) |
| `clearance_tracks.npz` | Executed tracks of Fig. 3; their RMSE and clearance match `mc_exclusions.json` |
| `spectral_*.json`, `spectral_field.npz`, `metrics.json` | Synthetic multispectral benchmark |

The generated acados code in `src/uav_nmpc_controller/acados_generated/` is used only by the ROS 2
node. Regenerating it with `make acados` reproduces all CasADi model and cost functions identically;
the solver templates come out under different file names and the generated `Makefile` holds paths
of the machine that produced it. The prebuilt `.so` files are x86-64 Linux binaries.

## 🧪 Limitations

- Rest-to-rest speed profile only; smooth turns were not evaluated. The disturbance is a world-frame
  force without airspeed coupling.
- Planning energy is essentially hover power times time and omits acceleration thrust; the
  closed-loop energy uses a different rotor model. Neither is a measured battery consumption.
- The airframe is not identified (thrust up to 26 × weight). Solve times are desktop times, the loop
  assumes zero computation delay, and attitude control is lost between 4 and 5 ms of delay.
- The NMPC has a perfect model, and the ablation matches reference information only approximately.
- Plans depend on the frame and on numerical tolerance (6.0–20.7 % over 72 rotations, 8.0–17.7 % for
  ±1 mm), and rows can skip narrow strips (free-field coverage < 0.98 in 135 of 600 plans).
- The Gazebo Classic mission is a single qualitative run.
